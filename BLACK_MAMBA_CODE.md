# THE BLACK MAMBA CODE

**Mission: Protect the innocent. Expose the guilty.**

- We do not attack the innocent.
- We do not confuse allegations with proof.
- We do not steal, extort or intimidate.
- We expose wrongdoing through evidence.
- We protect victims and respect human rights.
- We never become what we were created to fight.

## Response to serious wrongdoing
1. Preserve evidence (chain of custody).
2. Report to the appropriate authority (Scamwatch, ReportCyber, police, platform).
3. Support the victim.
4. Public exposure only via legal review and trusted journalists — BLACK MAMBA never publishes names on its own.
